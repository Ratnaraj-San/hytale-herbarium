# Herbarium — A plant journal for Hytale

Set out to discover the flora of Orbis! Herbarium adds a journal that
you fill in as you explore the world: every plant you come across is
recorded in it, along with hints on where to find it.

## Vanilla friendly

This mod is designed to blend seamlessly into the vanilla game.
Discoveries and progression tracking work much like the built-in
Memories collection system.

It is built entirely from vanilla assets — only a few of them were
slightly tweaked (the Journal's model, for example). A few custom
translations were also added, for texts the game does not provide.

---

## How to play

### 1. Craft your journal

At a workbench:

- 1 wood trunk
- 1 light leather

---

### 2. Open the journal

Right-click while holding the journal to unlock tracking of your discoveries.

You can also set the journal down: crouch and right-click while aiming at a
horizontal surface to place it in the world. Once placed, use the interaction
key on it to open it, and break it to pick it back up.

For each recorded plant, the journal tells you where to look for it:
caves, chests, Kweebec villages, Trork camps, the goblin realm, Howling
Sands ruins, and many more.

Your progress is tied to your character: it carries over from session
to session.

### 3. Explore and observe

Every new plant you discover is automatically added to your journal, sorted into one of 18 categories:
bushes, brambles, ferns, flowers, and much more.

### 4. Rewards

Every 50 plants recorded, you can collect a reward straight from your journal!

---

## In-game commands

OP perms are required.

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/herbarium ui` | Opens the journal without holding the item |
| `/herbarium reset` | Deletes all your progress |
| `/herbarium unlockall` | Unlocks every plant |

## Installing the mod

Drop the mod JAR file into your solo world's or your server's `mods` folder.
The mod is available in English and French only, for now.
